01Situation

Every channel had its own vendor.

Utilities sent notifications through separate vendors for SMS, IVR and email. Each campaign meant coordinating disconnected systems, with no shared templates, tracking or audit trail.

02Constraint

History lived in different places.

Schedules, delivery status and history lived in different places. As the client base grew to Alex Renew, MVU and Delco, which alone serves 50,000+ water customers, that setup couldn't scale.

04Decision

Put every vendor behind one interface, but keep channel choice and each delivery's status.

THE BOUNDARY One campaign. Separate receipts. A campaign is a shared instruction. Each channel retains its own delivery outcome, including failures.

WHO CHOOSES Channel choice stays with operators, because utilities know how their customers want to be reached.

  • One orchestration layer instead of a vendor tool per channel.
  • Mustache for templates, because logic-less templates stay fast and safe at high volume.
05Model

Retry the channel, not the campaign.

One send fans out to each selected channel, and each keeps its own receipt. Take SMS down, send, then retry SMS without resending IVR or email.
