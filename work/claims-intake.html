01Situation

Every claim was keyed in by hand.

Operations teams keyed in every claim by hand: read the paper, interpret it, type it. It was slow, error-prone and expensive, and it didn't scale.

02Constraint

Nothing recorded who changed what.

Routing was inconsistent. Claims had to route by rule, with a full audit trail for compliance.

03Ownership

Architecture & build.

I trained the OCR model and built the validation app, the routing rules and the audit trail.

04Decision

Automate extraction, and keep people responsible for checking it.

THE BOUNDARY Extraction is a proposal. The model extracts the fields. An encoder validates or corrects them before the claim routes by rule.
